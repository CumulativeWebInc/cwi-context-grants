      Honest limits.
      A grant binds the recorded context, not the universe — it is only as complete as what was put in. Hash verification proves the package is intact and unexpired; Ed25519 signature verification (verify-grant.js, Node) proves CWI sealed it. A VERIFIED grant does not prove the action was wise — only that this exact content, in this exact recorded context, was approved before expiry.
